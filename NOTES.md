## PRD "before you trust this file" — verification results

Checked against the installed source. Everything below is confirmed unless flagged.

**Confirmed as written**

- `mujoco_playground._src.mjx_env` exports `MjxEnv`, `State`, `step`, `get_sensor_data`,
  `get_qpos_ids`, `get_qvel_ids`, `render_array`, `MENAGERIE_PATH`.
- `mujoco_playground.wrapper.wrap_for_brax_training(env, episode_length, action_repeat,
  randomization_fn, full_reset)`.
- `mujoco_playground.registry`, and `manipulation.register_environment(name, cls, cfg_fn)`.
- `mujoco_playground.config.manipulation_params.brax_ppo_config(env_name, impl)`.
- `impl` **is** a config field. `rotate_z.default_config()` ships `impl='warp'`.
- `mjx.put_model(mj_model, impl=...)` and `mjx.make_data(model, impl=, naconmax=, njmax=, ...)`.
- `rh_` name prefix, `rh_forearm`, `rh_WRJ1`, `rh_A_FFJ0` (a *tendon* actuator). 20 position
  actuators, 24 joints.
- **`naconmax` is a total across worlds, `njmax` is per world.** The Leap config is
  `naconmax=30 * 8192, njmax=160` — i.e. naconmax scales with `num_envs`, njmax does not.

**Differs from the PRD**

| PRD says | Installed source |
|---|---|
| `mjx_env.init` | There is no `init`. It is `mjx_env.make_data(model, qpos=, qvel=, ctrl=, act=, mocap_pos=, mocap_quat=, impl=, naconmax=, naccdmax=, njmax=, device=)`. Note the extra `naccdmax`. |
| `learning/train_jax_ppo.py` under Playground | Installed as a **top-level** `learning` package: `site-packages/learning/train_jax_ppo.py`, not `mujoco_playground/learning/`. |
| Leap env "name like `LeapCubeRotateZAxis`" | Class `CubeRotateZAxis` in `_src/manipulation/leap_hand/rotate_z.py`; registry name is exactly `LeapCubeRotateZAxis`. |
| `randomization_fn(model, rng)` passed to PPO | The signature is `(model, rng) -> (batched_model, in_axes)`, but `BraxDomainRandomizationVmapWrapper` calls it as `randomization_fn(self.mjx_model)` — the rng must already be bound (Playground's train script does this with a partial). |
| Menagerie "fingertip bodies" / sites | Menagerie has **no fingertip sites**. The only site on the hand is `grasp_site` on `rh_palm`. We add five (see below). |

**PPO config.** The PRD's fallback table is close but not identical to the installed
`LeapCubeRotateZAxis` config. Use the installed one as the starting point:

| field | PRD fallback | installed |
|---|---|---|
| num_timesteps | 150_000_000 | 100_000_000 |
| discounting | 0.99 | 0.97 |
| num_envs / batch_size / unroll_length | 8192 / 256 / 40 | same |
| num_minibatches / num_updates_per_batch | 32 / 4 | same |
| learning_rate / entropy_cost | 3e-4 / 1e-2 | same |

**Still unverified — needs the GPU box (M2).** Warp-backend support for this scene:

## M0 — repo and scene

### Layout note

`assets/shadow_hand/` is the untouched Menagerie copy. `assets/right_hand_mjx.xml` is a derived
copy of `right_hand.xml` (same pattern Playground uses for `leap_rh_mjx.xml`), generated by a
scripted text transform with exactly four changes:

1. `meshdir` repointed at `shadow_hand/assets`.
2. `<option cone="elliptic" impratio="10"/>` removed — `scene.xml` owns simulation options.
3. `rh_forearm` given a mount `pos`/`quat` (fitted, see below).
4. Five fingertip sites added.

### Fingertip sites

Placed from the distal mesh geometry, not guessed. In each distal body's frame the mesh spans
z in [0.005, 0.032] (fingers) and [0.003, 0.035] (thumb), with the palmar pad at y ~= -0.005:

| site | body | pos |
|---|---|---|
| `rh_ff_tip` / `rh_mf_tip` / `rh_rf_tip` / `rh_lf_tip` | `rh_{ff,mf,rf,lf}distal` | `0 -0.006 0.026` |
| `rh_th_tip` | `rh_thdistal` | `0 -0.006 0.029` |

### `cap` vs `bottle` must be excluded explicitly

The cap sleeves over the neck by design, so the two geoms overlap by 16 mm permanently.
MuJoCo's `filterparent` flag does **not** remove this pair: the bottle has no joint, so it welds
to the world, and the parent/child filter skips world-welded parents. Without
`<exclude body1="bottle" body2="cap"/>` the cap sits permanently inside the neck geom and the
hinge fights a constant contact force. Added to `scene.xml`.

### Bottle neck lengthened

PRD starting values put the 32 mm-radius shoulder only 8 mm below the cap. At that geometry the
fitted pregrasp had the first fingertip **0.1 mm** off the shoulder — no room to gait. The neck
is now 20 mm half-height at bottle-local z=0.11 (was 12 mm at 0.102) and the cap at local
z=0.122 (was 0.106), which lifts the cap to 24 mm above the shoulder. 500 ml PET bottles have a
~40 mm neck, so this is if anything more realistic. World-frame geometry now:

| part | radius | world z |
|---|---|---|
| bottle body | 32 mm | 0.000 – 0.180 |
| neck | 13 mm | 0.180 – 0.220 |
| cap | 16 mm | 0.204 – 0.220 |

### Cap geom gets `priority="1"`

Contact friction is otherwise the elementwise max of the two geoms, so scaling fingertip
friction *down* in DR would have had no effect on hand/cap contacts. With the cap at priority 1
its `friction` and `condim=4` govern every hand/cap contact, which makes the DR friction knob
actually bite.

### Cap hinge: the frictionloss constraint leaks at MuJoCo's defaults

Measured on a cap-only model (bottle + cap, no hand), average cap speed over 2 s under a
constant applied hinge torque. `frictionloss=0.05`, `damping=0.002`:

| applied torque [N·m] | 0.01 | 0.03 | 0.049 | 0.055 | 0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PRD defaults** [deg/s] | 5.87 | 17.62 | 28.79 | 139.68 | 1396.40 |
| **with the fix** [deg/s] | 0.29 | 0.86 | 1.41 | 139.64 | 1396.40 |
| analytic `(τ-fl)/damping` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 139.63 | 1396.26 |

Above threshold the hinge is already exactly right. Below threshold it creeps: at a fifth of the
thread friction the cap still turns ~6 deg/s, which a gaiting policy could lean on instead of
actually unscrewing anything. The fix is on the joint:

```xml
solreffriction="0.01 1" solimpfriction="0.99 0.999 0.001"
```

20x less creep, breakaway unchanged, and an oscillating sub-threshold torque
(±0.04 N·m at 2 Hz for 5 s) now accumulates +0.000 deg instead of +0.021 deg.

Things that did **not** work, recorded so they are not retried:

- Stiffening further (`solimpfriction="0.999 0.9999 ..."` or `"0.9999 ..."`) makes creep
  *worse*, not better — 6.7 and 21.1 deg/s at τ=0.03. The constraint starts to jitter.
- `iterations=50 ls_iterations=50`: no change at all.
- `integrator="Euler"` instead of `implicitfast`: no change.
- `timestep=0.002` instead of 0.005: no change (5.87 -> 5.87).
- Armature does help (1e-2 gives 0.06 deg/s) but it is 20000x the cap's physical inertia and
  visibly distorts breakaway — at τ=0.1 the cap only reaches 252 deg/s in 2 s instead of 1396.
  Armature is left at the PRD's `1e-4`.

`tests/test_scene.py::test_cap_cannot_be_ratcheted_below_thread_friction` and
`::test_breakaway_speed_matches_the_damping_model` lock this in.

### Simulation options

`scene.xml` uses the PRD's fallback, not the Leap MJX scene's:

```xml
<option timestep="0.005" integrator="implicitfast" cone="elliptic" impratio="10"
        solver="Newton" iterations="10" ls_iterations="20"/>
```

The Leap MJX scene uses `integrator="Euler" iterations="5" ls_iterations="8"` with the default
pyramidal cone. Elliptic + `impratio=10` is what Menagerie ships for this hand, and
tangential/torsional friction fidelity is the whole point of a twisting task. Revisit at M3 if
throughput is poor, and at M2 for Warp support.

### Hand placement

Solved numerically by `scripts/fit_pregrasp.py` rather than hand-tuned, in three stages:
seed from Menagerie's own `grasp sphere` keyframe (whose thumb/first/middle tips already sit on
a ~14 mm-radius circle, close to our 16 mm cap), kinematic refinement with L-BFGS-B over the
mount pose and the 20 actuator targets, then a Powell polish whose objective **settles the
simulation first**, so it optimises the pose the keyframe actually holds rather than the one the
targets imply. Re-run with `--write` after any change to the bottle or cap geometry.

The settle stage matters: evaluated on the settled pose, the purely kinematic solution scores
1004.6 and the settle-polished one scores 45.4 on the same objective.

**Fitted result** (all five tests in `tests/test_scene.py` covering the M0 criteria pass):

| | thumb | first | middle | ring | little |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| radius from cap axis [mm] | 19.5 | 21.7 | 23.1 | 42.5 | 48.2 |
| height vs cap mid-plane [mm] | +1.8 | -2.0 | -2.5 | +27.3 | +33.9 |
| azimuth [deg] | -5.3 | -155.4 | +105.8 | +132.1 | +55.6 |
| **gap to cap surface [mm]** | **3.5** | **5.6** | **7.2** | 32.7 | 41.0 |

- Azimuthal separations of the three gaiting tips: **98.8 / 111.1 / 150.2 deg** — a well-spread
  tripod, thumb opposing first and middle.
- Ring and little are curled up and clear, 33–41 mm off the cap.
- Zero contacts at the pregrasp pose; holding the keyframe's own `ctrl` for 2 s leaves
  `max|qvel| = 0.0095 rad/s` and moves the cap 0.001 deg.
- Mount: `rh_forearm` at `pos="0.158327 0.0255187 0.552298"`,
  `quat="-0.0392589 0.941271 0.238297 -0.235971"` — the hand hangs from above and to the +x
  side, reaching down onto the cap, with the forearm clear of the bottle.

### Actuator `forcerange` (PRD M0 asks for this on the record)

Unchanged from Menagerie. This is the hard ceiling on fingertip force, so it bounds the cap
torque the hand can produce.

| actuator | kp | forcerange [N·m] |
|---|---|---|
| `rh_A_WRJ2` (wrist y) | 10.0 | ±10 |
| `rh_A_WRJ1` (wrist x) | 8.0 | ±5 |
| `rh_A_THJ5` | 0.4 | ±3 |
| `rh_A_THJ4` | 1.0 | ±2 |
| `rh_A_THJ3` | 0.5 | ±1 |
| `rh_A_THJ2` | 1.5 | ±1 |
| `rh_A_THJ1` | 1.0 | ±1 |
| `rh_A_{FF,MF,RF}J4`, `rh_A_LFJ5`, `rh_A_LFJ4` | 1.0 | ±1 |
| `rh_A_{FF,MF,RF,LF}J3` | 1.0 | ±1 |
| `rh_A_{FF,MF,RF,LF}J0` (tendon, J2+J1) | 0.5 | ±1 |

Sanity check against the PRD's own feasibility argument: 0.1 N·m at the 16 mm cap radius needs
~6 N of total tangential fingertip force. A distal joint capped at 1 N·m acting through the
~26 mm distal link gives ~38 N of normal force before saturating, so three fingertips at
friction 1.0 have ample headroom. The binding constraint will be grasp geometry, not torque.
